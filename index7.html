ABOUT

About StagioNest

StagioNest is a professional virtual staging service created to help real estate agents present empty properties in their best light.

I started StagioNest because empty rooms often fail to show a property’s full potential online. My focus is on realistic, MLS-friendly staging that helps buyers clearly visualize the space.

As a one-person studio, I personally handle every project — from reviewing your photos to delivering the final images. This means consistent quality, clear communication, and careful attention to every detail.

Whether you need a single room or a full listing, the goal is simple: natural-looking results that support your marketing and help your listings stand out.

Our editing process: We use digital and AI-assisted tools, with personal review of each project. Virtual furniture and décor are illustrations, not physical features of the property. Clients should follow their local MLS and listing-disclosure rules.

Terms & Conditions

Image Permission

You own the submitted photos or have permission to share them for editing.

Service

We provide AI-assisted digital virtual staging while preserving the property’s permanent features. After full payment, you may use the delivered images to market the property.

Pricing & Payment

Orders of 1–9 images cost $15 per image. Orders of 10+ images require a custom quote. Additional services are charged separately as agreed. All prices are in USD. We confirm the total price before payment.

Delivery

Standard delivery is 24–48 hours after payment and receipt of all required photos and instructions. Delivery for larger or complex orders is agreed separately. We will notify you of any delay.

Revisions

One revision round per image is included for adjustments within the original brief. Please send changes together within 7 days of delivery. New photos, a different design brief, or extra revision rounds may cost more, subject to your approval. Any errors on our part will be corrected at no additional charge and will not count toward your included revision round.

Cancellation & Refunds

After payment, change-of-mind cancellations and refunds are not accepted, except where required by law. If we cannot deliver the agreed service, we will refund the undelivered portion. Your statutory rights remain unaffected.

Listing Use

Please review the images before publishing and follow your applicable MLS and advertising requirements, including disclosure of virtual staging. Acceptance by every listing platform is not guaranteed.
