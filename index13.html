ABOUT

About StagioNest

StagioNest is a professional AI-assisted virtual staging service created to help real estate agents present empty properties in their best light.

I started StagioNest because empty rooms often fail to show a property’s full potential online. My focus is on realistic, listing-focused staging that helps buyers clearly visualize the space.

As a one-person studio, I personally handle every project — from reviewing your photos to delivering the final images. This means consistent quality, clear communication, and careful attention to every detail.

Whether you need a single room or a full listing, the goal is simple: natural-looking results that support your marketing and help your listings stand out.

Our editing process: We use digital and AI-assisted tools, with personal review of each project. Virtual furniture and décor are illustrations, not physical features of the property. Clients should follow their local MLS and listing-disclosure rules.

HELP & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send my property photos?

You can share a link to your photos using Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or WeTransfer. Please ensure the link is accessible before submitting the form.

What is your turnaround time?

Standard delivery is 24–48 hours after payment is confirmed and all required photos and instructions are received. Delivery for larger or complex orders will be agreed upon separately.

How many revisions are included?

One revision round per image is included for adjustments within the original brief. Please send all changes together within 7 days of delivery. New photos, a different design brief, or extra revision rounds may cost more.

How do I pay?

After reviewing your request, we will confirm the order details, total price, and delivery time by email, then send a Payoneer invoice. Work begins once payment is confirmed and all required photos and instructions are received.

Do I need to disclose that the property is virtually staged?

Please review all staged images before publishing. You are responsible for following the applicable MLS, listing-platform, and advertising requirements, including properly disclosing virtual staging. Requirements vary by MLS and platform and may include an on-image label, a photo-description disclosure, a dedicated disclosure field, and/or providing the original unedited photograph. Please check the applicable rules before publishing. Acceptance by every listing platform is not guaranteed.

Can I use the staged images for marketing?

Yes, after full payment, you may use the delivered images to market the property. We will not use your images in our portfolio or promotional materials without your permission.

What if I need object removal or decluttering?

We offer Object/Furniture Removal (+$10/image), Virtual Decluttering (+$10/image), and Complex Object Removal (+$15/image). You can select these add-ons directly on the order form. We will confirm the final total before payment.
